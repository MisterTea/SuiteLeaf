Privacy Policy

SuiteLeaf is built local-first and open source so your documents and AI prompts stay under your control.

Last updated: November 24, 2026
Plain-English summary: We do not sell your data, run ad trackers, or inspect your documents. In browser demo mode and desktop single-node mode, your files and on-device AI prompts never leave your device.

1. How SuiteLeaf Handles Your Data by Runtime Mode

Serverless / Browser Demo Mode

  • Documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and drawings are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB).
  • The built-in AI assistant executes locally in your browser using WebGPU / WebAssembly. Your prompts and document contents are not transmitted to SuiteLeaf maintainers.
  • Clearing your browser site data removes locally stored demo files, so export or download copies of anything you want to keep.

Single-Node Desktop Mode

  • Files are saved directly to your chosen local folder on your computer alongside a local SQLite index (~/.config/suiteleaf/library.sqlite).
  • Local AI inference runs on your device via llama.cpp. When you first enable a built-in model, the app downloads the model weights file once; your prompts and documents stay on your machine.
  • SuiteLeaf desktop does not require an account and collects no background usage telemetry.

Cluster (Self-Hosted Server) Mode

  • When you choose Connect to a server, your account credentials, SuiteDrive files, version history, and collaboration sessions are stored on the SuiteLeaf server(s) operated by you or your organization.
  • Multi-node clusters replicate data across nodes in that private cluster over encrypted libp2p connections. The cluster operator controls data retention, backups, and access policies.
  • If your cluster administrator enables OAuth sign-in (such as Google, GitHub, or Nextcloud), only the basic profile identifiers needed to authenticate your session (such as name and email address) are received from that provider.

2. Optional External AI Endpoints

SuiteLeaf lets you optionally configure an external OpenAI-compatible AI endpoint in assistant settings. If you explicitly enter a third-party endpoint and API key, prompts and relevant document context are sent directly from your client or server to the endpoint you configured, governed by that provider's privacy policy.

3. This Website (GitHub Pages)

  • This website is hosted statically on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server access logs (such as IP address and user agent) in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • This website uses no third-party analytics, no advertising pixels, and no tracking cookies.
  • We store only one local preference in localStorage (suiteleaf-theme) if you manually toggle between light and dark themes.

4. Open-Source Transparency

SuiteLeaf is open-source software licensed under the Apache-2.0 License. Anyone can inspect, audit, and build the complete source code on GitHub.

5. Contact & Updates

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and publish the change in the public repository. Questions or security/privacy reports can be opened via GitHub Issues.
