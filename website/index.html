Why SuiteLeaf

Built around privacy, real AI execution, and obsessive craft.

Most office suites force you to choose between cloud lock-in and bare-bones tools. SuiteLeaf brings modern AI agents and deep Microsoft Office & Google Workspace parity to software you control.

Privacy-First Local AI on Desktop & Mobile

Run capable language models directly on your own hardware—no mandatory cloud subscription, no training on your documents, and no data leaving your device.

  • Desktop: Native llama.cpp inference with FrogNano & Gemma GGUF models
  • Web & Mobile: On-device WebGPU & WebAssembly execution right inside the browser
  • Your choice: Keep everything offline, run on your team's server, or connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint

Smart Agents for Docs, Slides & Sheets

Instead of copy-pasting from a chat box, SuiteLeaf's agents use real editor tools to build, format, calculate, and organize your work step by step.

  • Docs: Draft structured reports, apply heading outlines, citations, footnotes & page setup
  • Sheets: Write live formulas, build charts & pivots, clean messy data, and add dropdowns
  • Slides: Create multi-slide decks, switch layouts, style backgrounds, and reorder slides

Painstaking Feature & UX Parity

Every workflow is measured against Microsoft Office and Google Workspace—down to keystroke counts, font metrics, and real-world community pain points.

  • Metric-identical Office fonts so imported DOCX, XLSX & PPTX layouts stay true
  • Deep editor features: live headers/footers, Roman front-matter, pivots, solver, suggestions & version diffs
  • 137 recorded storyboards continuously verifying real user journeys end to end
Get Started in Under a Minute

How to set up your SuiteLeaf account

Use SuiteLeaf solo with zero account required—or connect to your team's SuiteLeaf server whenever you want SuiteDrive sharing, real-time collaboration, and multi-device sync.

Narrated Walkthrough · Account Setup
Read video narration transcript

Setting up a SuiteLeaf account takes less than a minute, and your existing local files stay safe on your device. From your workspace home screen, click Connect to a server in the sidebar. Enter the address of your team's SuiteLeaf server—you don't even need to type https—and click Connect. SuiteLeaf opens the sign-in screen for that server. Click Create an account, enter your name, email address, and password, and submit. You land right in your team workspace with SuiteDrive ready for shared documents, spreadsheets, and presentations, while everything on your computer remains untouched whenever you disconnect.

1

Click "Connect to a server"

From your local SuiteLeaf home screen, choose Connect to a server in the sidebar. Local files stay right on your machine.

2

Enter your server address

Type your team's host name (SuiteLeaf tries HTTPS first automatically) and click Connect.

3

Create your account

Click Create an account and register with email & password—or sign in with Google, GitHub, or Nextcloud when enabled on your cluster.

4

Collaborate in SuiteDrive

Start sharing documents, spreadsheets, and decks across your team. Disconnect anytime to return to your untouched local library.

Flexible Architecture

One workspace. Three ways to run it.

Start in the browser with zero setup, install the native desktop app, or self-host a decentralized cluster for your whole organization.

Serverless Browser

Zero-install web workspace

Try SuiteLeaf right in your browser or install it as a PWA on desktop and mobile. Files autosave in local IndexedDB and on-device AI runs via WebGPU—nothing is uploaded.

Single-Node Desktop

Local files, native speed

Read and write directly to your own folder on macOS, Windows, or Linux with an instant SQLite index and atomic saves. Local AI runs privately through llama.cpp.

Cluster Mode

Self-hosted team collaboration

Run one or more Rust suiteleaf-node servers with SuiteDrive, real-time editing, version history, and encrypted peer-to-peer replication over libp2p.
