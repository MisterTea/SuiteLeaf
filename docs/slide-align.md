# Slide alignment

Align left, right, center, top, middle, or bottom moves the selected boxes to the shared edge of that selection. A box that is not selected stays where it is. A locked box stays where it is. One selected box is not moved by that command, because there is no second edge to meet.

Aligning one box to the slide puts it on the slide edge. On a 960 by 540 slide, a 100 by 40 title at x 10 moves to x 430 when centered. A locked title stays at x 10.

Distribute horizontal spaces three or more boxes with equal gaps. Boxes at x 0, 30, and 100, each 20 wide, become x 0, 50, and 100. The first and last stay. The slide toolbar Align menu uses the current selection. One box moves to the slide edge. Two boxes move to their shared edge. Distribute horizontally and Distribute vertically are on that menu and space three or more boxes. The menu was not opened in the browser, and two boxes were not shift-clicked there.

Lock on that menu keeps the box where it is. A locked title at x 10 stays at x 10 when a drag or an align asks for x 80. Its text can still change. Unlocking in the same change lets it move. A locked box is not dragged or resized. The menu was not opened in the browser. With an object selected, the arrow keys move it by 1. Shift plus an arrow moves it by 10. The other unlocked boxes in that group move by the same step. A group id that is only space does not group those boxes, so only the selected box moves. A locked box stays, and choosing a locked box does not move the group. With nothing selected, Left and Right still change slides. The slide canvas was not used for this key.

Bring forward paints a box one step later, which is on top of the box that was after it. Photo between title and note moves to the end. Send to back paints it first. Bring to front paints the title last. The list passed in stays title, photo, note. A box that is already at that end stays there. The menu items were not opened in the browser.

A box within 8 units of another box's edge snaps to that edge. A photo at x 14 next to a title at x 10 moves to x 10, and a green vertical guide is drawn at x 10. A photo at x 40 stays at x 40. On a 960-wide slide, a 100-wide box at x 426 snaps to the center, x 430, and the guide is at 480. The title passed in stays at x 10. The guide is cleared when the drag ends. The canvas was not dragged in the browser.

Shift-click adds a box to the selection. Group gives the title and the photo the id `beds`. The note stays out of the group. One selected box is not grouped. Dragging the title by 12 also moves the photo by 12, from x 40 to x 52. A locked photo stays at x 40. Ungrouping the photo also clears the title. The group id is stored on the box. The menu was not opened in the browser, and two boxes were not shift-clicked there.

Size and position reads x, y, width, height, and rotation. A rotation of 90 degrees is stored. A rotation that is not a number says `Enter a number for rotation.` and that value is not repeated. A rotation past 360 says `Keep rotation between 0 and 360.` A locked box keeps its rotation. Size and position reads x, y, width, and height. `80`, `20`, `100`, and `40` are accepted. A value that is not a number says to enter a number for x, and that value is not repeated. A width of 0 is refused. A locked box stays at x 10. The panel applies x 80 for an unlocked title and says the position was applied. A locked title is not moved, and the status does not contain `secret-value`. The Align menu includes Size and position. The fields follow the selected box. At `http://127.0.0.1:5173/` an Untitled presentation was opened. Align, then Size and position, showed the panel. After the title was selected, x was 100, y 160, width 760, and height 100. Setting x to 80 and choosing Apply position said the position was applied, and x stayed 80. Undo was then available. Opening the panel again showed x 100. The slide canvas was not measured.
