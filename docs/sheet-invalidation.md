# Formula invalidation

`cellsToRecalculate` names the formulas that read a changed cell, including a formula that reads one of those formulas. Changing `A1` when `B1` is `=A1` and `C1` is `=B1` returns `B1` and `C1`. `D1`, which is `=E1`, is left out. The cell value is not copied into the result, and the sheet is not changed.

A formula that refers to itself and one other formula is listed once each. A formula whose range is too large to list, such as `SUM(A1:A80)`, is included for any changed cell and marked unlisted, because the cells inside that range are not enumerated. Changing `B1` still includes that formula. `cellsToRecalculate` does not calculate the formulas. `recalculateDirty` copies a direct reference, adds a `SUM` whose range is small enough to list, and rounds one cell. A halfway value rounds away from zero, so `=ROUND(-1.5, 0)` is `-2` and `=ROUND(-15, -1)` is `-20`. `=ROUND(1.5, 0)` stays `2`. `=AVERAGE(A1:B1)` of 10 and 30 becomes 20. A blank cell is left out of that average and out of `COUNT`, `MIN`, and `MAX`. `=COUNT(A1:A2)` is 1 when A2 is blank. `=MIN(A1:B1)` is 10 and `=MAX(A1:B1)` is 30. An average that reads text stays at its previous value. `=TRIM(A1)` removes extra spaces. `  Beds   North  ` becomes `Beds North`. `=DATE(A1, B1, C1)` of 2026, 1, and 15 becomes `2026-01-15`. 31 February stays at its previous value. Text is not copied into the address list. `=YEAR(A1)`, `=MONTH(A1)`, and `=DAY(A1)` read an ISO date. ` 2026-01-15 ` is year 2026, month 1, and day 15. A day that is not on the calendar, such as 31 February, stays at its previous value. Text is not copied into the address list. `=INDEX(B1:B2, 2)` returns the second cell, and a position outside the range becomes `#REF!`. `=MATCH(A1, A1:A2, 0)` returns 1 for an exact match and `#N/A` when the value is missing. `Beds` matches `beds`. The address list does not copy the cell text. `=OFFSET(A1, 1, 0)` reads the cell one row down. `=INDIRECT(C1)` reads the cell named by the text in C1. Text that is not a cell address is `#REF!`. A step that is not a whole number is `#VALUE!`. `=RAND()` uses the draw the caller supplies. A draw of 0.25 stays 0.25. `=RANDBETWEEN(1, 3)` with a draw of 0.5 is 2. A low bound above the high bound is `#NUM!`. A draw outside 0 inclusive to 1 exclusive stays at its previous value. `=MEDIAN(A1:D1)` of 10, text, 4, and 30 is 10. Four numbers 10, 4, 30, and 20 have median 15. A range with no number is `#NUM!`. Text is skipped. `=TODAY()` is the UTC day, such as `2026-11-03` when the clock is `2026-11-03T15:04:05.000Z`. `=NOW()` is `2026-11-03T15:04:05Z`. A clock that is not a time stays at its previous value. `=TEXT(A1, "0.00")` of 8.9 is `8.90`. `=TEXT(A1, "0")` of -1.5 is `-2`. `=TEXT(A1, "#,##0")` of 1234 is `1,234`. `=TEXT(A1, "0%")` of 0.5 is `50%`. A format this pass does not know stays at its previous value. `=LEFT(A1, 4)` of `Beds North` is `Beds`. `=RIGHT(A1, 5)` is `North`. `=MID(A1, 6, 5)` is `North`. A negative count is `#VALUE!`. `=CONCATENATE(A1, B1)` joins the two cells. `=INT(A1)` of 8.9 is 8, and of -1.2 is -2. `=SQRT(A1)` of 9 is 3. A negative root is `#NUM!`. `=MOD(A1, B1)` of -10 and 3 is 2. A divisor of 0 is `#DIV/0!`. `=POWER(A1, B1)` of 2 and 3 is 8. A power that is not a finite number is `#NUM!`. Text stays at its previous value. `=PRODUCT(A1:C1)` of 10, text, and 4 is 40. Text is skipped. A range with no number is 0. `=COUNTA(A1:C1)` counts filled cells, including text, and skips an empty cell. `=XLOOKUP(A1, B1:B2, C1:C2)` searches one range and returns the cell in the other. `beds` beside `Beds` and 10 returns 10. No match becomes `#N/A`, or the extra cell when one is named. The ranges must be the same length. `=HLOOKUP(A1, B1:C2, 2)` searches the first row. `North` returns the number under that header. A missing header becomes `#N/A`. A row number past the range stays at its previous value.
