# Replace the speaker note

Who: Nia found "pears" in the speaker notes of Welcome. The slide itself says Market. She wants the note to say "apples" instead, and she wants Market to stay Market.

Inspired by: Google Slides' Replace, which changes a note without saying the change is in the notes. The canvas looks the same, so she cannot tell whether the note changed.

## Scenes

1. Nia is on Prices. She finds pears. The notes on Welcome open, the bar says the match is in the notes, and the words are not covered. She types apples in the replace field. Replace says it will change this note. One click. The note becomes "mention the apples". The canvas still says Market.
2. Thanks also has "pears" in its notes and not on the canvas. After the first replace, the bar is on that remaining note. Replace all says Replace all 1. One click. Thanks' note becomes "mention the apples". The bar says 1 replaced. Both canvases are unchanged.
3. She finds pears again. The bar says no matches. The notes stay "mention the apples".
4. She saves, returns home, and reopens. Both notes still say apples. Market is still on the Welcome canvas.
5. A skipped slide's note is replaced too. Presenting still skips that slide.

## Google Workspace benchmark

- Slides can replace text in speaker notes, but the bar does not say the change is in the notes.
- Replace all does not show the count of note matches before the click.
- It is easy to think the canvas changed because the notes pane is easy to miss.

## Better than Google Workspace when

1. Replace on a notes match changes only that note, and the bar says the change is in the notes. The canvas text stays.
2. After one note is changed, the bar moves to the remaining note match. Replace all shows the count first.
3. Replace all changes every remaining note match and says how many. Canvas words stay.
4. "pears" matches "Pears", and the replacement keeps the capitalization she typed. "apples" stays lowercase.
5. A search with no matches does not change notes, and Replace all is not offered.
6. Save and reopen keep the new notes and the original canvas words. Zoom and slide numbers do not change.
7. A skipped slide's note is replaced in the editor. Presenting still skips that slide. Canvas replace still changes a word on a slide.

## Success

- Nia changes the note to apples, sees that the change was in the notes, and Market stays on the slide.

## Gaps

- Find includes `slide.notes` and the status says notes are not replaced.
- Replace and Replace all still change canvas matches only.
