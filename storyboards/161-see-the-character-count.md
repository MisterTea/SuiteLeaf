# See the character count

Who: Nia is keeping the market minutes under a character limit as well as a word limit. "Apples were four dollars." is 4 words. She also needs the characters, including the spaces, and the count with the spaces left out.

Inspired by: Google Docs' Tools › Word count dialog, which lists characters and characters excluding spaces. Those numbers stay inside the dialog unless she opens it again.

## Scenes

1. Nia opens a new document. The corner says 0 words and 0 characters. She types "Apples were four dollars." The corner says 4 words, 25 characters, and 22 without spaces. She does not open a dialog.
2. She selects "four dollars". The corner says 2 of 4 words and 12 of 25 characters. The sentence stays in the document.
3. She clicks away. The corner returns to 4 words, 25 characters, and 22 without spaces.
4. She saves, returns home, and reopens. The corner still shows those counts. She downloads Word. The file contains the sentence and does not contain the label "25 characters".

## Google Workspace benchmark

- Docs lists characters only inside Tools › Word count.
- Characters excluding spaces is a second line in that same dialog.
- The live word count, when enabled, does not show characters.

## Better than Google Workspace when

1. Words, characters, and characters without spaces are visible together while she types. There is no dialog.
2. "Apples were four dollars." is 4 words, 25 characters including the period and the spaces, and 22 characters without spaces.
3. Selecting "four dollars" says 2 of 4 words and 12 of 25 characters. Clicking away returns to the document totals.
4. An empty paragraph does not add a character. The existing word count still ignores it.
5. Characters inside a table cell count, including spaces.
6. Save and reopen show the same counts. The counts are not stored as a paragraph.
7. The downloaded Word file contains the sentence and does not contain "25 characters" or "22 without spaces".

## Success

- Nia sees 4 words, 25 characters, and 22 without spaces without opening a dialog.

## Gaps

- The status bar shows the live word count from storyboard 159. It does not show characters.
- Character totals still require the statistics dialog from Tools.
