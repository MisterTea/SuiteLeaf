# 81 · Put a picture behind the text

**Mode:** single-node · **App:** Docs · **Video:** [videos/81-put-a-picture-behind-the-text.mp4](videos/81-put-a-picture-behind-the-text.mp4) · **Script:** [scenes/81-put-a-picture-behind-the-text.spec.ts](scenes/81-put-a-picture-behind-the-text.spec.ts)

## Who and why

Lena wants the stall photo as a pale backdrop behind the market announcement, with the words still readable on top. She also gets Word files where a logo is already in front of the text. Storyboard 70 can put a picture beside the paragraph. A picture set behind or in front of the text is still dropped, and the import note says so.

## Inspired by

- [Wrap text in Google Docs](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/10274561): In front of text and Behind text let the picture float over or under the paragraph.
- Storyboard [70](70-put-a-picture-beside-the-text.md): the further-gaps note says a picture behind or in front of the text is dropped on import.
- People lose a watermark or a stamp when a Word file opens and that picture disappears.

## Scenes

1. **The announcement.** Lena opens a short note. The photo is in the line with the words.
2. **Behind.** She selects it and chooses Behind text. The words stay where they were and draw on top of the photo. She can still read them.
3. **Move it.** She drags the photo so it sits under the heading. The words do not move with it.
4. **In front.** She chooses In front of text. The photo covers part of a line, and that line is still in the document underneath.
5. **Back.** In line puts the photo back in the sentence, and the words flow with it again.
6. **From Word.** She opens a .docx whose picture is behind the text. The picture is behind the words, not missing, and the import note does not say it was dropped.
7. **Save.** She downloads the SuiteLeaf file and opens the copy. The photo is still behind the heading.

## Google Workspace benchmark

Google Docs offers In front of text and Behind text in the same wrap menu as inline and wrap ([Docs Help](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/10274561)). Behind text is hard to select again, because the words are on top and the picture has no obvious handle. People click the text and cannot get the picture back.

## Better than Google Workspace when

1. Behind text and In front of text are choices on the selected picture, next to In line, Left, Right, and Break text.
2. Behind text keeps the words readable on top of the picture.
3. Dragging a behind or in-front picture moves only the picture. The words stay put.
4. The picture can be selected again after it is behind the text, without deleting the words.
5. In line puts it back in the sentence.
6. A Word picture that is behind or in front of the text opens that way, and the import note does not say it was dropped.
7. The choice survives save and reopen.

## Success looks like

The announcement is readable on top of the stall photo. Lena can drag the photo, bring it back into the sentence, and open a Word file that already had the picture behind the words.

## Gaps and notes

- Behind and in front are not choices, and Word import drops those pictures.
- Do not remove In line, Left, Right, or Break text.
- Do not rebuild crop or page size.
