# 140 · Put the rota in the club folder

**Mode:** cluster · **App:** Files · **Video:** [videos/140-put-the-rota-in-the-club-folder.mp4](videos/140-put-the-rota-in-the-club-folder.mp4) · **Script:** [scenes/140-put-the-rota-in-the-club-folder.spec.ts](scenes/140-put-the-rota-in-the-club-folder.spec.ts)

## Who and why

Rosa wrote the watering rota at the top of My Drive, then made a Garden club folder. In Google Drive she would open Move, expand a folder tree, and hope she picked the right place. If she picked wrong, there is no Undo. In the document itself, SuiteLeaf does not show which folder the file is in, so she cannot tell from the title.

## Inspired by

- Storyboard [23](23-start-a-document-in-suitedrive.md): the editor does not show the file's folder, and new files from the editor land at the top of My Drive.
- [Move files and folders in Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2375091): Move opens a folder tree. The file leaves the current folder.
- [r/googleworkspace: how to organize Drive without a giant folder maze](https://www.reddit.com/r/googleworkspace/comments/1tcbtc4/how_to_organize_google_drive_without_creating_a/): people lose files in trees they cannot see at a glance.

## Scenes

1. **Two places.** Rosa is signed in. My Drive shows "Watering rota" and a folder named Garden club. The rota is not inside the folder.
2. **The folder is on the file.** She opens the rota. Next to the title, a control says **My Drive**.
3. **Move.** She clicks **Move**. A dialog lists the folders by name, including Garden club. It is not a tree she has to expand. She chooses Garden club. That row is marked. She confirms. A banner says the file moved there, with **Undo** and **Dismiss**, and the banner stays.
4. **Undo.** The control next to the title says **Garden club**. She clicks **Undo**. It says **My Drive** again, and the banner is gone.
5. **The folder.** She moves it to Garden club again and clicks **Garden club** next to the title. The folder opens and lists Watering rota. My Drive does not.

## Success looks like

- The open file shows its folder, and that name opens the folder.
- Move lists folders by name and puts the file there.
- Undo puts it back.
- After a move, My Drive and Garden club agree about where the file is.

## Better than Google Drive

- Folders are a list of names, not a tree she has to open level by level.
- The folder stays visible next to the title.
- Undo puts the file back. Google's Move does not offer that.

## Gaps and notes

- Recorded. Rosa signs in. My Drive lists Watering rota and Garden club, and Garden club does not list the rota. In the open rota, the control next to the title says My Drive. Move lists Garden club by name. She chooses that row, which stays marked, and Move here puts the file there. A banner says it moved to Garden club, with Undo and Dismiss, and the banner is still there after a few seconds. The control then says Garden club. Undo makes it My Drive again. She moves it once more and clicks Garden club. That folder lists Watering rota, and My Drive does not. No browser dialog.
- The same Move dialog is on the file row, so a file can be moved without opening it. This recording uses the editor.
- The banner stays until Undo or Dismiss. It does not follow her into the folder, so she undoes before she leaves.
- Viewers and commenters see the folder and can open it. They do not see Move. Someone who can only respond to a form does not see Move either.
- A folder she can only view is left off the list. My Drive is a choice when she owns the file and it is not already there. A nested folder is one row, such as Garden club / Meetings, not a tree she has to open.
- New and Make a copy put the new file in the open file's folder. See storyboard [141](141-start-the-next-rota-in-the-same-folder.md).
