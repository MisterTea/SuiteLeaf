# 20 · Build a presentation

**Mode:** single-node · **App:** Slides · **Video:** [videos/20-build-a-presentation.mp4](videos/20-build-a-presentation.mp4) · **Script:** [scenes/slides-cluster.spec.ts](scenes/slides-cluster.spec.ts)

## Who and why

Priya volunteers at a community garden and has ten minutes to put together slides for Saturday's kickoff meeting. She has used Google Slides and PowerPoint and expects the same basics: a title slide, new slides from a layout menu, a splash of color, and dragging slides into order.

## Inspired by

- W12 Slides tutorial: multi-slide deck, layouts, backgrounds, shapes, reordering thumbnails ([docs/workflows-and-expected-behavior.md](../docs/workflows-and-expected-behavior.md)).
- [Google Slides: Add, delete & organize slides](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1694830): "New slide with layout", and reorder by dragging slides in the left panel.
- [Google Slides: change the theme, background, or layout](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1705254): background for one slide or the whole deck, and Layout to rearrange a slide.

## Scenes

1. **New presentation.** From the home screen Priya clicks "New presentation". A title slide opens, and the title box reads "Untitled presentation".
2. **Name it.** She types "Spring Garden Kickoff" in the file title box.
3. **Title slide.** She replaces the title and subtitle text with "Spring Garden Kickoff" and "Community Garden · Saturday 10 am".
4. **Title & body.** "+ New slide" → "Title & body" adds slide 2. She types "This season's plan" and three bullets.
5. **Two columns.** "+ New slide" → "Two columns" adds "How you can help" with Saturday jobs on the left and May events on the right.
6. **Section header.** "+ New slide" → "Section header" adds a dark "Questions?" slide.
7. **Shape.** On the title slide she clicks Circle, sets Fill to Yellow, and drags the shape into the top-right corner as a sun.
8. **Background.** With nothing selected she picks Background → Sage green. The slide and its thumbnail turn soft green.
9. **Reorder.** She selects the "How you can help" thumbnail and clicks Move slide up, so it comes before "This season's plan".
10. **Autosave.** The status reads "Saved on this device".

## Success looks like

- Four slides in the order Title, How you can help, This season's plan, Questions?
- The title slide has a sage background and a yellow sun shape; the section slide is dark.
- The presentation saved itself without a Save button.

## Gaps and notes

- New slides contain real text, not placeholders: "Slide Title", "• First key point…", "Click to add subtitle", and the file title on a new deck's title slide. Priya has to select all and retype each box, and any box she skips shows its stand-in text in the slide show. Google Slides and PowerPoint use placeholders that disappear.
- Slides reorder only with the Move slide up / Move slide down buttons. Dragging thumbnails and keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl/Cmd+Up) don't work, unlike Google Slides and PowerPoint.
- Layout on an existing slide changes nothing visible. Switching a new title slide to "Two columns" left both text boxes exactly as they were.
- Background and Theme apply to the current slide only. There is no "apply to all slides" (Google's "Add to theme").
- A new shape lands at a fixed spot on top of the text and has to be dragged clear. Fill offers 8 presets (mostly pastels) and no custom color.
- The Line tool, Transition, Grid view, and the Menus search button do nothing when clicked.
