# 138 · Hand someone the sign-up link

**Mode:** cluster · **App:** Forms · **Video:** [videos/138-hand-someone-the-sign-up-link.mp4](videos/138-hand-someone-the-sign-up-link.mp4) · **Script:** [scenes/138-hand-someone-the-sign-up-link.spec.ts](scenes/138-hand-someone-the-sign-up-link.spec.ts)

## Who and why

Maya put the plot sign-up form on the club's server. The neighbors do not have SuiteLeaf accounts. In Google Forms she would open Share, then General access, then "Anyone with the link," and still wonder whether that lets them edit the file or only fill it in. She wants a link that only adds a sign-up, and she wants to be able to close it when the plots are full.

## Inspired by

- Storyboard [137](137-let-someone-else-sign-up.md): a teammate with an account can respond. There is still no public link.
- Storyboard [27](27-share-a-document-with-a-teammate.md): the share dialog says only the people listed can open the file.
- [Share files from Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494822): "Anyone with the link" is a general-access setting, separate from the list of people, and it can mean view or edit.
- [r/techsupport: share to anyone with the link](https://www.reddit.com/r/techsupport/comments/iuclvn/google_drive_share_to_anyone_with_the_link/): people cannot tell who the link lets in.

## Scenes

1. **The form.** Rosa is signed in and opens "Plot sign-ups". The questions are already there.
2. **The sign-up link.** She clicks **Sign-up link**. The dialog says this link only adds a response. It does not show other people's answers, and it does not change the questions. The address is on the page. She clicks **Copy**.
3. **A neighbor opens it.** She opens that address. There is no sign-in. The page is the sign-up, with Your name, Plot size, and Notes. There is no Questions editor and no list of other people.
4. **Alex signs up.** She enters Alex, chooses Large, and submits. The page says the sign-up was received.
5. **The row is in the form.** She goes back to the form. Responses shows Alex and Large. The questions are unchanged.
6. **She closes the link.** She opens Sign-up link and clicks **Turn off**. The same address now says the sign-up is closed. Alex's row is still in Responses.

## Success looks like

- One control makes a link that only accepts a response.
- Opening it does not ask for an account and does not show other responses.
- The new row is in the same form.
- Turning the link off stops new responses and keeps the ones already there.

## Better than Google Forms

- The link is named for signing up. It is not "anyone with the link" buried under general access.
- It cannot be confused with permission to edit the file or read the answers.
- Closing the link is one button, and it does not delete the sign-ups.
