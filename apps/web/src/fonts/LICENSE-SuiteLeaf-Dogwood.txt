SuiteLeaf Dogwood is built from VenturisSansADF-Regular 1.005;FFEdit, Copyright © Arkandis Digital Foundry, from modifications to the Utopia fonts distributed through the TeX User Group (http://tug.org/fonts/utopia) under the same license as the Utopia fonts themselves -See LICENSE-utopia file-.

SuiteLeaf changed it so text lays out exactly as in Harrington: outlines were scaled to that font's
units per em and moved sideways to center them in their advance; advance widths, kerning, glyph classes,
and line metrics were set from that font's layout. Where that font joins letters into a ligature, a
ligature glyph was made from this font's own letters, moved closer together. No outline shape was
otherwise changed.

Characters the source font lacks were filled with glyphs from these fonts, scaled and centered
the same way (counts are across it). Each keeps its own license, reproduced below; all are
compatible with the license of the whole font.

- 6 glyphs from KoHo (OFL-1.1). Copyright 2018 The KoHo Project Authors (https://github.com/cadsondemak/Koho)
- 3 glyphs from Noto Sans Math (OFL-1.1). Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/math)
- 3 glyphs from Noto Serif (OFL-1.1). Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic)
- 5 glyphs from Roboto Flex (OFL-1.1). Copyright 2017 The Roboto Flex Project Authors (https://github.com/TypeNetwork/Roboto-Flex)

The build scripts are in scripts/fonts/ of the SuiteLeaf repository.
